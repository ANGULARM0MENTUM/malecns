    What is actually playing

    The nodes are cell types from the public male Drosophila CNS connectome, not a cartoon of all 166,700 neurons. Visual types see each empty intersection. Synapses among those types, their strongest visual relays, the male courtship types, and descending neurons then settle for eight steps. Acetylcholine excites. GABA and glutamate inhibit. A linear readout, fit on this frozen circuit, ranks the squares. The synaptic weights were not trained.
